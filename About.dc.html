01 · Company Overview

A Canadian Mining Company, Advancing Tungsten in Northern Portugal

Allied Critical Metals Inc. (CSE: ACM · OTCQB: ACMIF · FSE: 0VJ0) is a Canadian mining company focused on developing tungsten projects in northern Portugal. The Company's flagship Borralha Tungsten Project and its Vila Verde Tungsten Project are both 100%-owned, positioning Allied among the largest undeveloped tungsten resource holders in the European Union.

Mission & Strategy

Our strategy is to advance Borralha toward production using its past-producing infrastructure and historic resource base, while continuing to define Vila Verde's exploration upside. The goal: a secure, Western-controlled source of tungsten supply, developed responsibly and delivered to market ahead of Western demand growth.

02 · Why Tungsten

A Critical Metal, Concentrated in the Wrong Hands

Tungsten is essential to defense, aerospace, electronics and manufacturing, valued for its hardness and heat resistance. Today, production is dominated by China, Russia and North Korea — leaving Western economies exposed to supply-chain risk. Allied's Portuguese projects offer a politically stable, EU-based alternative.

Geographic Focus

Both projects sit in the Braga District of northern Portugal — an established mining jurisdiction with existing infrastructure, skilled labour, and a stable political and regulatory environment within the European Union.
