Borralha Tungsten Project

Overview

Borralha is Allied's 100%-owned flagship tungsten project in the Braga District of northern Portugal, held under a mining rights concession license. The advanced-stage brownfield project carries an updated mineral resource estimate effective November 19, 2025, and unique high-grade, ferberite-hosted metallurgy that supports a low-cost production profile.

Strategic Importance

Borralha has been endorsed by IdD Portugal Defence, designating it a project of strategic national importance — reflecting tungsten's role in defense-critical supply chains and the project's contribution to Western supply security.

Past-Producing Legacy

Historic production from 1904 to 1985 leaves existing infrastructure and geological data that materially reduce the technical and permitting risk of redevelopment compared to a greenfield project.
