01 · Company Overview

A Canadian Mining Company Focused on Western Tungsten Supply

Allied Critical Metals is a Canadian mining company advancing tungsten development in northern Portugal. Tungsten is a critical metal used in defense, aerospace, electronics and manufacturing — and its supply is currently dominated by production in China, Russia and North Korea. Allied's flagship Borralha project and its Vila Verde property together represent one of the largest undeveloped tungsten resource bases in the European Union.

02 · Key Investment Highlights

100% Ownership

Full ownership of both Borralha and Vila Verde, with no underlying royalties or joint-venture overhang.

Past-Producing Asset

Borralha produced wolframite concentrate from 1904–1985, reducing technical and permitting risk versus greenfield projects.

Strategic Financing

Backed by multi-million dollar financing packages structured to accelerate development toward production.

Nasdaq Application

Filed to list on the Nasdaq Capital Market in August 2026, expanding reach to U.S. institutional investors.
